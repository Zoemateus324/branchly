# Pricing — Branchly

Branchly is a reputation intelligence platform for multi-location businesses.

## Free
- Price: $0/month
- Locations: 1 location
- Features: Google review monitoring, basic dashboard, limited AI replies (5/month)
- Support: Community

## Starter
- Price: $19/month (billed monthly) | ~$15/month (billed annually)
- Locations: Up to 3 locations
- Features: Unlimited AI review responses, competitor benchmarking, weekly reports, email alerts
- Support: Email

## Pro
- Price: $29/month (billed monthly) | ~$23/month (billed annually)
- Locations: Up to 10 locations
- Features: Everything in Starter + advanced competitor benchmarking, location heatmaps, sentiment analysis, API access, 14-day trial
- Support: Priority email + chat

## Premium
- Price: $49/month (billed monthly) | ~$39/month (billed annually)
- Locations: Up to 50 locations
- Features: Everything in Pro + white-label reports, custom integrations, dedicated account manager, SLA
- Support: Dedicated account manager

## Enterprise
- Price: Custom — contact sales@branchly.com.br
- Locations: 50+ locations (unlimited)
- Features: Everything in Premium + custom SLA, SSO, API rate limits lifted, onboarding support
- Support: Dedicated team
